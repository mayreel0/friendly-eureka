# iOS Surfaces

The iOS pilot owns the QR-invoked App Clip ARKit path and the first merchant recorder spike.

## App Clip Spike

- Verify QR invocation into the App Clip.
- Verify ARKit world tracking starts inside the App Clip target.
- Record the App Clip size budget and minimum iOS target in `docs/ar-device-test-matrix.md`.
- If the App Clip cannot host the AR guidance runtime, fall back to an installed iOS guest app for the pilot and keep QR entry as the session launcher.
